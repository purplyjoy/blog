Scenery

Sometimes, the simplest moments bring the deepest sense of peace. In a world full of noise, responsibilities, and endless thoughts, finding a place where the mind can rest feels truly priceless. For me, that sense of comfort comes from witnessing breathtaking views — quiet, beautiful scenes that allow me to pause, breathe, and feel free from the weight of my worries.

I remember when I was a child, we would often go to the black lava (a tourist spot in Albay) near our house, and admire the beautiful view of Mayon Volcano, along with the city stretching below it. It was one of our favorite ways to spend time with friends — simply sitting, talking, and enjoying the scenery. After all, who wouldn’t love gazing at the famous Mayon with its almost perfect cone? Even though I've lived in Albay my entire life, I never get tired of admiring its beauty. Each time I see it, the volcano still feels majestic and awe-inspiring, as if I'm seeing it for the first time.

Living in a province, I am surrounded by trees and mountains. I always see shades of green and blue, but what would it be like to experience a new environment? Like a field full of colorful flowers, specially tulips. The Netherlands has been my dream country ever since. I hope to one day visit their famous tulip fields and walk through the rows of vibrant flowers, experiencing a beauty different from what I've always known at home.

For now, since I can't afford to travel yet, I will continue admiring the views around me. Whether it's a simple scene or a majestic one, both will always catch my eye and fill me with wonder. Loving the beauty in things is simple — it only takes a moment of pause to notice and appreciate it.

Memories

Memorable moments in life are meant to be treasured and remembered. We never know what experiences await us in the future, but it is important to value what we have already encountered. Even the simple things we accomplish help shape who we are today and who we will become in the future.

My family used to live in a traditional nipa hut, a small house that could easily be swept away by a strong typhoon. So, whenever there is a coming disaster we all pack our things and wrap them in plastic. I remember how devastated I was when I forgot about my photo album from Grade 7. It was soaked in water and all the photos I've kept were all ruined. I really loved that photo album because it was a gift from my friend when we had our own exchange gift on Christmas. That's when I became more sentimental about the gifts I received because I don't know when I'll lose them. Since then, I always keep all the important things in a storage box and make sure it will not be wet by the rain. In that box, all the memorable things in my life are kept. I have a new photo album where I will make sure from now on not to get soaked in water. I also kept all the letters I received from my friends. Some of the notebooks and papers I used during high school are also in there. Everything that I want to keep are piled up, including my diaries I used back from my elementary and high school days.

While I was looking through that storage box, I saw and reread all the things I had done before. I realized that I used to be such an enthusiastic person, and all those happy moments were written in my diary. Even though I no longer write in a physical notebook, I still keep my thoughts in the notes on my phone. Reading them brought back so many memories and allowed me to feel the same emotions I felt during those times. I truly loved being a child, and I will always cherish that phase of my life.

Another way I preserve my memories is through Google Drive, where I upload photos and videos that I want to keep for the long term. I organize everything into separate folders, so I always know exactly where to find the photos or videos I want to look at and remember. Every small event in my life, no matter how ordinary it may seem, becomes meaningful when I capture it, and I make sure to upload it to Google Drive. This way, I can revisit those moments anytime and reminisce about the experiences, the people I shared them with, and the feelings I had at that time. Over the years, my collection has grown into a personal archive of memories— a digital photo album that reminds me of my journey, my growth, and the countless moments that have shaped who I am today.

Everything I have experienced in life has taught me valuable lessons and helped me grow into the person I am today and who I will become in the future. The memories I have kept will always serve as reminders of my journey and motivate me to keep working hard toward my dreams. I have set a goal of collecting even more memories and organizing them into carefully arranged photo albums. Each memory will have its own place and in their respective folders for different types of memories, so each album is organized based on what the photos are about, making it easier to revisit and reflect on the moments I’ve captured. These memories will not only preserve my past but also inspire me to pursue my dreams and embrace new experiences that I have longed for, reminding me that every step of my journey matters.

Diary

Events in our lives, whether they are big and unforgettable or small and simple, can always be written down in a diary. For me, keeping a diary is something meaningful and comforting. It allows me to capture moments that might otherwise fade with time. Even the ordinary days—when nothing extraordinary seems to happen—become special once they are written on paper, because they represent a part of my journey.

Writing in my diary is both enjoyable and therapeutic. It provides a safe space where I can openly share my thoughts, feelings, and experiences without worrying about being judged. At the end of each day, I like to sit down and reflect on everything that happened—what made me smile, what challenged me, and what lessons I gained. Through this habit of writing, I learn more about myself and become more aware of my emotions.

I often reread what I have written in my diary. When I do, I am reminded of the person I was during those times. It feels like revisiting a younger version of myself, seeing how much I have grown and changed. Sometimes I laugh at my old thoughts, and sometimes I feel emotional remembering moments I had forgotten. My diary becomes a time capsule of my life, preserving memories that I can look back on whenever I want to reflect on my journey.

Dream Careers

From the moment I became aware of the world around me, I have carried a dream in my heart. I have experienced many things and still hope to experience even more. Although I have not yet achieved my ultimate goal, I have had a meaningful journey in life—one that I continue to carry with me to this day.

When I was a child, I dreamed of becoming a teacher, a lawyer, a businessman, or even a housewife. Those were a lot of dreams for such a small child. But there's nothing wrong for dreaming big. I cherished those childhood dreams deeply.

I initially dreamed of becoming a teacher. Since most of my family on my father’s side are teachers, it naturally became my dream job as well. My aunties were all teaching in the same school when I was in elementary. They became my teachers, and I truly admire their passion for their work. Seeing how dedicated they were at school made me interested in teaching. I would often teach younger kids in our neighborhood, and I genuinely enjoyed doing it. That childhood dream made me value education and shaped the way I see learning today.

Becoming a lawyer was also one of my dream jobs. As I observed how justice works in our society, I began to question how can we truly achieve equality. For me, being a lawyer was a way to promote fairness within the justice system. I wanted to help those who could not afford legal support by explaining the law to them and helping them understand what is right and wrong. Many people are not even aware when their human rights are violated by those in higher positions, and it hurt me that I did not have the power to do anything about it. Also, I had this favorite television series that I would always watch during weekends. It's about bringing justice and equality to everyone. They would reenact the experiences of the characters, portraying situations where their rights are violated. I specially loved the ending of each story because the characters finally received the justice they deserved. I truly admired the lawyers in the series who guided them, encouraged them to be strong, and helped them face their fears in order to overcome their challenges. I did not pursue law because I felt that my abilities were not suited for that path. I had already chosen my dream course when I entered high school. Even though I did not follow that direction, I know there are people who share the same value, and I will always admire those who continue to stand for justice and promote equality.

As a child, I always thought being a businessman was the best job. I admired businessmen in their neat clothes and confident way of speaking. In high school, whenever we were tasked to research successful entrepreneurs, I will always learn lesson from their life stories. They motivated me to also become a businessman. I imagined starting small, learning from every challenge, and eventually creating something that could make a difference in people’s lives. Although I didn’t choose a business-related course, I still believe it’s possible to become a businessman in the future. I can use the knowledge and skills from my degree, start small, and gradually achieve success that I can later use to build my own business.

This may seem unusual to some people, but when I was a child, I dreamed of becoming a housewife. I wanted to build my own family and give my future children the experiences I didn't get to have as a child. I imagined myself as a sweet and caring wife who would patiently wait for her husband to get home from work. I was inspired by fictional stories where the main character faced many challenges in life, but eventually overcame them and met the love of her life who she will build a family with. Those stories made me believe that love, family, and resilience were beautiful goals to have. I believed that being a housewife was still a job, because it requires effort, sacrifice, patience, and unconditional love. That is why I included it as my dream career when I was young. Even now, I still dream of becoming a housewife someday, but my perspective has grown as I have grown. Before, I imagined myself stayiny at home and focusing only on raising my children. Now, I want to work alongside my future husband. I want to be not only a loving wife and mother, but also a responsible woman who contributes financially, built her own career, and supports her family in every way possible.

At last, my current dream career: becoming a web or software developer, or pursuing any IT-related profession. Since I am taking up Information Technology, it feels natural that I would also build my future career in this field. Ever since I learned about computers and the things they made possible, I became curious. That curiosity eventually led me to this path. I still remember the first time I used a computer. I was in Grade 4 when our school built a new computer laboratory, and we were the first batch of students to use it — at least, as far as I can remember. The only thing I clearly remember doing was learning how to properly open and shut down a computer. I don't recall the other activities, but I do remember how amazed I was by how everything worked. Unfortunately, we didn’t have our own computer at home, so I wasn’t able to explore more or deepen my understanding of how computers work and the science behind them. When I reached high school, I was excited to become a Grade 9 student because that was when we could finally choose our specialization in TLE (Technology and Livelihood Education). In Grades 7 and 8, we had exploratory courses, where each quarter introduced us to a different field. But in Grades 9 and 10, we were allowed to choose our specialization. Of course, I chose CSS (Computer Systems Servicing) because it focused on computers. However, things did not go as I had hoped. Near the end of the 2020 school year, when I was in Grade 8 and about to enter Grade 9, the COVID-19 pandemic happened. Instead of face-to-face classes where I could have used the school’s computer laboratory, we shifted to online classes and printed learning modules. I didn't get the chance to learn more about computers since I didn't have my own computer. Sometimes, I feel like those two years of high school were lost opportunities — years that could have been spent developing my skills. We only had a chance to use the computer lab when there was a project about connecting wires, and that was the only thing we did during those times. Thankfully, face-to-face classes returned when I entered senior high school. This time I chose STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) strand instead of CSS or ICT. I was told about the advantages of STEM, so I decided to take that path. And now, here I am in Bicol University, my dream school, taking up my dream course, Information Technology. Throughout my two years in this course, I have learned so much about computers, and the world behind them. What once felt like magic slowly became knowledge that I could understand and apply.Throughout my two years in this course, I have learned so much about computers and the world behind them. What once felt like magic slowly became something I could understand. Each lesson answered my curiosity and helped me become more certain about my dream career in IT. Now that I am clearer about my goals, I want to continue to learn and apply my knowledge in the real world. Although I am still learning, I have begun collecting ideas and envisioning projects that I look forward to develop into websites of my own. My goals remind me why I started and motivate me to keep growing. There are many opportunities in the field of technology, and I believe this journey is only just beginning. I am excited to keep learning, creating, and turning my curiosity into something meaningful.

All the dreams I had growing up remind me of who I once was and who I am still becoming. The goals I held in my heart tell the story of why and how I am here today. They make me more determined to keep striving for the future I once imagined. To the child who carried so many dreams, I know you are excited to see who I will become. Your dedication and courage brought me to this path. I will always be grateful to you, for shaping the person I am today and for everything I have achieved so far.

The Child I Was

There was a time when all I wanted was to grow up. I wished I could fast-forward into the future, skip waiting, and become the adult I imagined. I thought adulthood meant freedom—the chance to make my own decisionos, live on my own, and finally build the peaceful, stable life I dreamed of. As a child, I couldn't wait to leave childhood behind.

But somewhere along the way, that dream changed. Now, I find myself wishing I could go back. I wanna go back to being a kid. Back to being the loud, carefree child who laughed without thinking twice and found happiness in the simplest things. A kid who spent afternoons playing until the sun went down, threw trantrums over little things, and looked forward to Christmas gifts. Back then, life felt lighter. There were fewer worries, fewer expecations and pressuer, fewer burdens to carry. Growing up gave me the freedom I once longed for, but it also came with responsibilities I never truly understood as a child. Looking back, I realized how much I've changed. I'm no longer the loud and lively kid I used to be. The child who once couldn't wait to become an adult is now an adult who quietly misses being a child.

I remember how I used to spend my days playing from morning until night. Most of my childhood was spent outside with friends, making our own adventures. We'd play hide-and-seek, play house, run in the rain, splash around in the mud, and pretend we're chefs by mixing leaves and flowers into our own little recipes. I think I've played all the games that children play. I was an adventorous kid. I would wander off to places my parents didn't knwo I had fone. I often go to the rice fields not too far from our home. We would walk there under the bright afternoon sun, picking fruits and flowers along the way. Sometimes, we'd even come home carrying a bag full of mangoes. Those simple trips became some of my favorite childhood memories. That was our way of spending time together, creating fun out of the ordinary.

I used to have a small bike that I rode everywhere whenever my friends went somewhere. It was one of my most treasured childhood possessions because receiving gifts was rare for our family. I didn't have many toys growing up, so having that bike made it incredibly special to me, and I cherished it with all my heart. One day, I tagged along with my older sister as she went to a neighboring barangay to print her homework. She walked with her classmates while I happily rode my little bike beside them. Feeling proud, I offered to carry the freshly printed paper in the small basket at the front of my bike. I thought I was helping. As we were going downhill, one of her male classmates suddenly stepped in front of me and blocked my way. I couldn't stop in time. Thankfully, I only crashed into a pile of sand, but my sister's paper was badly crumpled. I remember feeling embarrassed and upset. After that day, I stopped tagging along whenever she was with her classmates. I preferred staying with my own friends instead. Looking back, that memory reminds me that childhood isn't made up of happy moments alone. We often remember childhood as carefree and full of laughter, but it also comes with moments of embarrassment, disappointment, and even resentment. As children, our emotions feel so intense. I genuinely hated that boy for what happened, and for a while, that memory stayed with me. Now, years later, I can't even remember who he was. It's funny how the people who once seemed so important in our childhood slowly fade into strangers, while the feelings they left behind become lessons instead. What once felt like a big, unfair moment has become just another story that makes me smile.
